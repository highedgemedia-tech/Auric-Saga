How do I know which consultation to choose first? +

If your struggle relates to your home or office space (restlessness, stagnant revenue, insomnia), start with Vastu. If you need timing and life milestones (marriage, career pivot, wealth cycles), choose Astrology. For acute emotional decisions, pick Tarot. Alternatively, choose "Help me choose" on our booking form.

Do Vastu solutions require structural demolition? +

Never. At Auric Saga, we practice non-destructive Vastu. We balance directional imbalances using color resonance, metal energy conductors, mirror alignments, and sacred geometry.

How are online consultations conducted? +

All sessions occur through a private, encrypted Google Meet video or voice call. You will receive an invitation link prior to the appointment with instructions on what notes or floor plans to have ready.

What if I do not have my exact birth time for Astrology? +

If your minute of birth is uncertain, we synthesize your reading via Sacred Numerology and Prashna Shastra (Horary Astrology) to maintain uncompromised accuracy.
